What do the details mean?

Passenger class & fare
Class describes the ticket category. Fare is the recorded ticket price in pounds at the time, without adjustment for inflation.
Family aboard
Count siblings and spouses separately from parents and children. These fields do not include every type of relative or travelling companion.
Port of embarkation
Southampton in England, Cherbourg in France, or Queenstown in Ireland, now called Cobh.
Unknown details
Age is missing for 177 of the 891 records. For unknown age or fare, the model uses a typical value learned during training; an unknown port uses its most frequent training category.

How to read your estimate

A 70% estimate means the model assigns a probability of 0.70 to survival for those details. It is not a verified personal chance or a reconstruction of what happened. The model learns patterns from a limited historical sample; it cannot account for every event, decision, or circumstance aboard.

Try changing one detail at a time to explore how the estimate responds. This shows the model's behavior, not what would have happened if a real passenger's circumstances had changed.
